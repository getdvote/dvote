Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Welcome to dvote. These terms explain how the dvote app and its loyalty programme work. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use dvote.

1. What dvote is

dvote lets you collect loyalty points at participating coffee shops ("shops") and redeem them for rewards those shops offer. Each shop runs its own programme: it decides how many points you earn for what you spend and which rewards it offers.

2. Your account

  • You must give accurate details and keep your sign-in private.
  • One account per person. Accounts are personal and cannot be transferred.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else is using it.

3. Earning points

  • Show your QR code before paying. Shop staff scan it and enter your bill; the points are added to your card for that shop.
  • Points are calculated from the shop's current rule and rounded down. A minimum purchase may apply.
  • QR codes are single-use and expire after a few minutes. A screenshot or copy of an old code will not work.
  • Points earned at one shop belong to that shop's card only and cannot be moved to another shop.

4. Redeeming rewards

  • You choose when to redeem. The reward's points are taken from your card when the shop confirms it.
  • Rewards, their prices in points and their availability are set by each shop and may change.
  • Rewards have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

5. Fair use

Do not misuse dvote, for example by sharing codes, faking purchases or trying to get points you did not earn. We and the shops may review unusual activity, correct points added by mistake, and suspend accounts that break these terms.

6. Shops

Each shop is responsible for its own products, prices, opening hours and rewards. dvote provides the app and records points, but is not the seller of anything you buy at a shop.

7. Changes to the programme

We may update the app, these terms or how the programme works. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app. A shop may also change or end its programme; we will try to give you notice where we can.

8. Your privacy

We keep your personal details (name, email, phone, gender, birthday) private. Shops see only what they need to give you points and rewards, never your name, email or phone. More details will be set out in our Privacy Policy.

9. Closing your account

You can stop using dvote at any time. If your account is closed, any points on your cards are lost.

10. Liability

We work to keep dvote running and your points accurate, but the app is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, dvote is not liable for indirect losses or for the loss of points caused by events outside our control.
